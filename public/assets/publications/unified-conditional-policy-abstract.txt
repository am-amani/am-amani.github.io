Title: A Unified Conditional Policy for Multi-Robot Navigation via LiDAR-to-Vision Distillation

Authors: Amir Mahdi Amani, Samaneh Amani, Alireza Hossein MajidiRad
Venue: Machines (MDPI), Accepted for 2026

Short Abstract:
This work presents a conditional policy framework for multi-robot navigation that transfers behavior from LiDAR-based perception to vision-based perception through teacher-student distillation. The approach aims to preserve robust navigation decisions under sensing changes while maintaining coordination quality across multiple robots.

Keywords: multi-robot navigation, LiDAR, vision, policy distillation, teacher-student learning
