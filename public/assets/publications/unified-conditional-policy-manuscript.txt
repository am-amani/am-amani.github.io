Title: A Unified Conditional Policy for Multi-Robot Navigation via LiDAR-to-Vision Distillation

Authors: Amir Mahdi Amani, Samaneh Amani, Alireza Hossein MajidiRad
Status: Accepted
Journal: Machines (MDPI)

Manuscript Summary:
This manuscript presents a unified conditional policy for multi-robot navigation with cross-modality policy distillation. A teacher policy trained from LiDAR observations supervises a student policy that operates on vision observations, enabling robust navigation behavior under sensing changes. The framework is designed to preserve control quality and coordination under practical perception variability.

Core contributions:
- Conditional policy design for multi-robot control
- LiDAR-to-vision teacher-student distillation
- Robust navigation behavior under modality shifts

Keywords:
multi-robot navigation, policy distillation, LiDAR, vision, teacher-student learning
