This publication introduces a conditional control framework for multi-robot navigation where policy behavior is transferred across sensing modalities.

The method uses distillation to preserve navigation quality when transitioning from LiDAR-based inputs to camera-based observations, with a focus on consistency and robustness in collaborative scenarios.

Key points:

- Conditional policy formulation for cross-modality behavior transfer
- Teacher-student distillation between LiDAR and vision representations
- Emphasis on robust multi-robot behavior under sensing variability
